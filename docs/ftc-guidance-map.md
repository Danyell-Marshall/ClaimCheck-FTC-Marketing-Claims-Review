# Rule catalog and FTC sources

These are **editorial prompts** informed by public FTC guidance, not legal interpretations of particular advertisements. Whether a claim is deceptive depends on full context, substantiation, consumer interpretation, and applicable law.

| Rule ID | Pattern category | Why an editor should look closer | FTC reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ABSOLUTE_PROMISE` | Guarantees, absolute outcomes | Check the promise's scope and support | [Advertising substantiation policy](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation) |
| `EARNINGS` | Specific earnings / income representations | Check support, typicality, material expenses and context | [Advertising FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business) |
| `CUSTOMER_EARNINGS` | Relative customer financial outcomes (e.g., doubled income) | Check the evidence, number of customers represented, and whether the results are representative | [Advertising substantiation policy](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation) |
| `HEALTH_CURE` | Health benefit, proof or approval language | Check claim-specific scientific support and exact terminology | [Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) |
| `TYPICAL_RESULTS` | Testimonials and personal results | Consider atypical results and truthful endorsements | [FTC Endorsement Guides FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) |
| `ENDORSEMENT_DISCLOSURE` | Affiliates, sponsorship and material connections | Verify clear, conspicuous connection disclosures | [FTC Endorsement Guides FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) |

## Critical reminders

The FTC emphasizes that advertisers generally need a reasonable basis for objective claims **before** they make them. Whether a disclosure is effective depends on placement, prominence, and the overall ad—not just the presence of a keyword. Material connections between endorsers and marketers can warrant clear disclosures. Some kinds of health claims require particularly rigorous scientific support. These principles motivate the questions above, but none is reducible to a keyword match.

**Not included:** Truth-testing, evidence appraisal, net-impression analysis, image and video review, claims implied only by context, layout assessments, complete industry-specific law, current enforcement guidance monitoring, or a legal verdict.

Sources were checked against official FTC pages during project preparation in November 2026. Rules and links should be reviewed periodically; guidance can change.
